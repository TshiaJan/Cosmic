## 1. Introduction
Welcome to **AI Cosmic Civilization** ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled when you download, install, and play AI Cosmic Civilization on mobile devices and tablets.

## 2. Information We Do Not Collect
AI Cosmic Civilization is designed as a client-side simulation and offline-first casual management experience:
- **No Personal Identification:** We do not collect names, email addresses, physical addresses, phone numbers, contacts, or passwords.
- **No Account Creation:** The game does not require user registration, logins, or social profile authentication.
- **No Location Tracking:** We do not access or collect GPS coordinates or precise location data.
- **No Biometric or Camera Access:** The game does not request or access camera, microphone, or biometric sensors.

## 3. Data Stored Locally On Your Device
All gameplay progression, galaxy parameters, civilization milestones, resource counters, unlock states, audio/visual preferences, and cosmic ratings are stored strictly on your local device (using client-side local storage or app sandboxed storage).
- You can reset or erase this data at any time by clearing the application's cache/data via your device settings or by uninstalling the application.

## 4. Advertising & Third-Party Service Providers
If the game integrates third-party advertising networks (such as Google AdMob) or mobile analytics to support free play:
- Third-party SDKs may automatically collect non-personal device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID / IDFA), device model, OS version, language settings, and general interaction data for ad serving and fraud prevention.
- Users can manage or reset ad tracking preferences directly through their device settings (e.g., *Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID* or *Opt out of Ads Personalization*).

## 5. In-Game Virtual Currencies & Purchases
Any references to in-game currency, resources (such as Dark Matter, Zero-Point Energy, Dilithium Crystals, or credits), subscriptions, or cosmic tiers within the game are purely simulated gameplay mechanics. Real-money transactions, if offered, are handled securely through official platform billing systems (e.g., Google Play Billing). We never receive or store your payment card details or financial information.

## 6. Children's Privacy
AI Cosmic Civilization is rated for general audiences (Everyone / PEGI 3) and does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).

## 7. Data Security
Because no personal user data is transmitted to or hosted on external developer servers, the risk of data exposure through external server breaches is eliminated. Your local data remains secure within your device's sandboxed environment.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect app enhancements or statutory updates. Any revisions will be reflected by the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.
